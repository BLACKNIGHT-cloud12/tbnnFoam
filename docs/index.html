Why Galilean Invariance Matters

Standard naive neural networks taking raw coordinates or velocity gradients change their predictions if you rotate or translate the coordinate frame. By restricting inputs to scalar invariants and outputs to Pope's tensor basis, tbnnFoam guarantees mathematically identical turbulence stresses in any reference frame.

Prandtl Secondary Flow Generation

Standard Boussinesq linear models assume v'² = w'², meaning normal stress difference is strictly 0. TBNN's 2nd basis T⁽²⁾ = S² - ⅓tr(S²)I produces non-zero (v'² - w'²), which physically forces the 8 corner counter-rotating vortices.
