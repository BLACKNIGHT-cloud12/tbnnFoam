1. Invariant Feature Inputs

Unlike classical neural networks that train on raw spatial coordinates, TBNN takes the 5 frame-invariant scalars of strain S and rotation R, guaranteeing identical predictions under any grid rotation.

2. Deep Nonlinear Synapses

Each neuron computes y = LeakyReLU(∑ w_i x_i + b). The 3 dense layers learn the highly nonlinear coupling between mean shear and turbulent normal stress imbalances.

3. Output Tensor Recomposition

The 10 output weights g⁽ⁿ⁾ scale the 10 Pope basis matrices T⁽ⁿ⁾. Specifically, basis T⁽²⁾ drives the non-zero (v'² - w'²) that generates the 8 secondary flow cells!
